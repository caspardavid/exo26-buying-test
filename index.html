Your trio and your brief

Teams work in trios by number: 01·02·03, 04·05·06, 07·08·09, 10·11·12, 13·14·15. In each trio, the lowest number takes brief A, the next B, the highest C.

Round · 4 minFounder pitchesBuyer decidesReviewer asks

The buyer plays the buyer from the founder's brief. A team missing from your trio? Work as a pair and take turns as the reviewer.

A · Missed appointments

Buyer: a clinic operations lead

Is reminder outreach worth funding for one service?

Test within €200: show a fictional appointment list and two reminder scripts. Ask how empty slots are counted and who owns the budget.

B · Rehab benefit

Buyer: an insurer or employer benefits lead

Which members, and which outcome, would justify a pilot?

Test within €200: mock up a one-page benefit offer. Compare a fee per eligible member with a fee per active user. Ask what would trigger renewal.

C · Care coordination

Buyer: a family caregiver paying directly

Which coordination task is worth a monthly fee?

Test within €200: show a fictional shared calendar with a paid coordination option. Ask for a concrete next step at a stated price.

1Prepare your pitch

6 minutes · on your brief

Choose your buyer, one assumption, a price and a baseline: what happens today, without you? Then draw the money and the data, in two colours.

AI can write two competing offers or a fictional scenario (prompt 1 below). Keep anything AI made apart from what a real person said.

2Pitch, buy, review. Then rotate.

3 rounds × 4 minutes

Founder

Pitch your offer in 90 seconds, then answer the buyer.

Buyer

Name your budget line. Say yes, no, or "yes, if…". Real buyers have a boss and a reason to say no.

Reviewer

Ask: what would you need to see to change your decision?

Pick your team number above to see who plays what in each round.

3Now your own 200€ venture

8 minutes

Design one real test you can run before 1 December, with what's left of your 200€.

  • The action must cost the buyer something: money, a signature, a booked meeting. "I'd use it" doesn't count.
  • Decide the threshold now, or every result will look like a reason to continue.
  • The role-play answers were rehearsal, not evidence.

4Your buyer line

4 minutes

It writes itself from step 3. Copy it, then paste it into your week 8 log, under "What we tried".
